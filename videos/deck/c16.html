16Pre-scaling

Add server copies and upgrade the database before a spike you know is coming.

Think of it as

Calling in extra staff before the lunch rush, not during it.

Use it for

Any planned event
Scenario. The snow forecast airs on the 6 PM news. Tire-shop traffic will jump 20x in 2 minutes.
Without it
  1. Autoscaling notices the busy servers at 6:06
  2. New copies are ready at 6:09
  3. 4 minutes of errors at the worst moment
With it
  1. 5 PM: 2 to 6 copies, bigger database, CDN warmed
  2. 6:05: the spike arrives; the system is already sized
Why it worksYou know when the spike is coming, so use that.
"Autoscaling reacts in minutes. The spike arrives in seconds."

Related terms

AutoscalingThe cloud adds copies when servers get busy. Keep it as a safety net.
WorkersScale them on queue length, not CPU.
