20Load test and game day

A load test fires fake traffic at 2 to 3x the peak at a test copy, with a pass bar. A game day rehearses: flip switches, fake outages.

Think of it as

A dress rehearsal before opening night.

Use it for

Before every launch
Scenario. Two days before a doorbuster sale.
Without it
  1. A missing index is found at 6 AM on sale day
  2. On-call has never practiced pausing orders
With it
  1. k6 simulates 120,000 visitors, 1,000 buying one TV
  2. Pass bar: 50 sold per deal, 0 double charges, p95 under 800 ms
  3. Run 1 fails (missing index); fix, rerun, pass
  4. Game day: flip every switch, fake Stripe errors, time the response
Why it worksYou find the missing index on Tuesday, not at 6 AM on Friday.
"Pass means 0 oversold, 0 double charges, and p95 under 500 ms."
