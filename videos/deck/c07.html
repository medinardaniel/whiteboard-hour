7Sweeper and reconciliation jobs

Programs on a timer that clean up. The sweeper releases stuck things. Reconciliation compares your records with an outside source and fixes gaps.

Think of it as

Closing-time checks: count the till against the receipts.

Use it for

Expired holdsStuck ordersMatching payments
Scenario. 50 pizza customers reserve, then abandon the Stripe page. One paid order's webhook never arrives.
Without it
  1. Abandoned pizzas stay reserved: lost sales
  2. The paid order is never cooked
With it
  1. Every minute, the sweeper finds expired holds
  2. It asks Stripe 'paid?'; if not, the pizzas go back on sale
  3. Nightly: compare Stripe's payments with our orders, fix the missing one
Why it worksSome messages always get lost. These jobs make the system end up correct anyway.
"Every minute, release expired holds, after asking Stripe whether they were paid."
