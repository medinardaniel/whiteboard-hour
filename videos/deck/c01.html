1CDN

Servers around the world that keep copies of your site's files (pages, images, scripts) and serve each visitor from one nearby.

Think of it as

Local warehouses stocking your product, so not every order ships from the factory.

Use it for

Every designFirst spike defenseFiles that rarely change
Scenario. A bike shop's site gets shared on a forum: 50,000 visitors in an hour, about 2 MB each.
Without it
  1. Every page, photo and script comes from the shop's 2 servers
  2. They max out, and pages crawl
With it
  1. First visitor per region: 1 fetch from the shop
  2. Everyone after: served by a nearby CDN server
  3. Shop servers only see cart and checkout
Why it worksThe files are the same for everyone, so don't make your servers send them every time.
"The CDN takes most of the read spike before it reaches us."
