6Queue + workers

A to-do list for slow work. The server writes a note and replies at once; background workers pick up notes and do the work.

Think of it as

Order tickets on a kitchen rail: the cashier doesn't cook.

Use it for

EmailsTextsImage processingAnything that can lag
Scenario. Each paid pizza order sends a confirmation text. The SMS provider takes 1 to 2 s and sometimes times out.
Without it
  1. The customer waits on the text before seeing 'confirmed'
  2. A provider timeout becomes a checkout error
With it
  1. API queues 'text order 42' and confirms instantly
  2. A worker sends the text, retrying on failure
  3. After 5 failures: dead-letter queue and an alert
  4. 900 orders in 30 min: texts lag minutes, none lost
Why it worksCheckout stays fast, and a slow provider can't break ordering.
"At-least-once delivery, plus workers that are safe to run twice."

Related terms

At-least-onceA note may be handed out twice, so jobs must be safe to repeat.
