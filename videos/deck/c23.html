23Security basics

Five things in one breath: HTTPS, secrets in a vault, the provider handles cards, least privilege, little personal data.

Think of it as

Lock the doors, keep the keys in a safe, let the bank hold the cash.

Use it for

When asked
Scenario. The interviewer asks: 'What about security?'
Weak answer
  1. 10 minutes on encryption algorithms
  2. No time left for go-live
Strong answer
  1. HTTPS everywhere
  2. Stripe key and DB password in a secrets manager
  3. No card numbers, thanks to hosted checkout
  4. Text worker can read the queue, not the database
  5. Store only a name and phone number
Why it worksIt shows you know the basics, in 20 seconds.
"HTTPS everywhere, secrets in a vault, no card data on our servers, and each service gets only what it needs."
